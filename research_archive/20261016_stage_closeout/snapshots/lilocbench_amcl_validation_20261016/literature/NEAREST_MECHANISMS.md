# Narrow nearest-mechanism check (not a new-method claim)

1. [LILocBench, IROS 2025](https://www.ipb.uni-bonn.de/pdfs/trekel2025iros.pdf), experimental setup and pose-tracking sections checked in full paper. Its original baseline pose-tracking protocol uses GT once for initialization and then no trajectory alignment. Published results support the existence of long-term-change tracking challenges but do not prove our own implementation has reproduced the same failure. Its 10-repeat results and example submission are different evidence; the latter starts with large initial errors, unlike our registered nearby-pose initialization.
2. [ROS1 AMCL 1.17.3 original laser code](https://github.com/ros-planning/navigation/blob/f13af47ee2bca6c3bf99db2965e46e55303f6e66/amcl/src/amcl/sensors/amcl_laser.cpp): already implements common-particle beam consensus and full-observation fallback. An adaptive rejection/consensus/threshold switch alone is not a research contribution. We do not alter these formulas.
3. [Akai, Reliable Monte Carlo Localization, JFR 2023, author preprint](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2205.04769), sections 4.5.1 and 6.3.1 checked: mapped/unmapped observation classes are marginalized per candidate pose; robust localization and observation classes are estimated jointly. The author explicitly discusses loss of correction capability when initial estimates are far from truth. Thus the broad robust-rejection-versus-pose-correction dilemma is already prior art. We have not established a remaining ability beyond this work.
4. [Official als_ros](https://github.com/NaokiAkai/als_ros): README explicitly confirms Ubuntu20.04/Noetic and separates class-conditional tracking, reliability estimation, global pose sampling and MRF failure detector. Default tracking enables robust localization plus reliability; global sampler is a separate option. README-level compatibility is not local build/execution qualification. No als_ros clone, compilation or run is performed before the AMCL evidence gate. If later needed, isolate the class-conditional model and match the omni motion model while controlling the other switches.

Unknown: whether class-conditional MCL suffices on the particular remaining intervals in this experiment; its local compatibility and fairness are not inferred from public availability. There is no current novelty claim, training, neural map, active planner or new robust-weight mechanism.
